Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Best pot size: 4 to 6 in / 10 to 15 cm wide terracotta or ceramic pot.
      Painted band: 1 to 1½ in / 2.5 to 4 cm tall around the outside.
      Let the pot dry completely before adding a plant or water nearby.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Paint one tiny hidden heart, leaf, or dot on the back of the band as a quiet Team Crafties signature.

            Safety and plant care

              This pattern decorates the outside of a plant pot only. Keep paint and sealer away from soil, roots, edible herbs, and drainage holes.
