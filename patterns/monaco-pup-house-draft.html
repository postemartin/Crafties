Real dog house

Signature details

This little house keeps the details that make the Monaco version special: a thick oval base, tall Ferrari-red walls, a cream entrance trim, two black roof panels, gold edging, and sweet extras like the bone sign and side shield. It is meant to read like a tiny champion’s house, not just a decorative prop.

💜 Nath note: because this house is real-dog size, firmness matters more than perfection. Use a very sturdy super bulky yarn, keep your fabric tight, and do not skip the non-slip mat or cushion support if your pup will really use it.

      Before you start

      Finished size on the poster is about 28 in / 71 cm wide, 22 in / 56 cm deep, and 20 in / 51 cm tall. The entrance opening is about 12 in / 30 cm wide by 9 in / 23 cm tall, and the sheet says it fits dogs up to 25 lb / 11 kg.
      The instructions below are cleaned up from the pattern poster so they are easier to read while you crochet. Keep the nearby reference image open if you want to match the decorative placement very closely.
      For a real dog-size build, think beyond stitch counts: watch floor stability, wall firmness, washable inserts, and whether your yarn choice will hold shape over time. If your pup is a chewer or scratcher, add a washable inner support and supervise first use.
