Sea Stingray Pouch

A cuddly royal blue velvet stingray with a white belly that opens as a pouch — perfect for storing tiny sea creature friends. Blue top panel with white embroidered spots, white crocheted eyes, separate pink crocheted cheeks, and a white bottom panel joined around the edges with the mouth left open.

      Before you start

      This stingray is made with Bernat Baby Blanket yarn and a 10 mm hook. The body is two flat panels — a blue back with white embroidered spots and crocheted cheeks/eyes, plus a white belly. They are joined around the edge with an open mouth. There is NO stuffing — the stingray is completely flat.
      The body is worked in ROWS (not rounds). You chain 19, then single crochet back and forth, increasing at the first and last stitches to widen the wings, then decreasing to narrow toward the tail. Make one blue piece (back) and one white piece (belly).
      The eyes and cheeks are crocheted pieces (magic ring of 6 sc), not safety eyes or embroidery. Sew them onto the blue back panel. The white spots are embroidered with a needle.
      The tail is a separate piece: chain 25, sc in 3, 2 sc in each st until 2 last, sc in last 2. This creates a natural curl. Attach it to the tail point of the body.
