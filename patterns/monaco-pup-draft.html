Monaco Pup

A closer Crafties-style rewrite for the golden racing dachshund. This pass follows the original poster shape more carefully: longer nose, lower body, seated back legs, front legs tucked under the chest, and the cream-and-red harness sitting higher around the shoulders.

What we are keeping

The original dog has a long gentle dachshund body, a rounded forehead, a nose that projects forward, long floppy ears, and a chest harness that frames the shoulders instead of sitting like a belly band. This rewrite leans into those exact details.

💜 Little Nath-style note: the look changes a lot depending on leg placement. Keep the front legs more vertical under the chest, angle the back legs slightly backward for the seated pose, and do not overstuff the tail or ears.

      Before you start

      Finished size is about 10 in / 25.5 cm long and roughly 6.5 in / 16.5 cm tall if you use bulky yarn and a 4.0 mm hook, or a little smaller with lighter yarn.
      To get closer to the original dog, think in sections: rounded head first, then neck, then long body tube, then the rear and tail shaping. The front legs stand straighter; the rear legs are shorter-looking because they sit angled backward.
      This page has been desk-tested for round counts and shaping logic against the reference sheet, but it has not been physically stitched in yarn by Hermes. Final real-world tweaks may still happen after a hands-on sample.
