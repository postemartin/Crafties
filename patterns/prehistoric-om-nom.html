Prehistoric Om Nom Crochet Plush

A caveman-style green amigurumi plush with dark green spots, two little arms, two stubby feet, three tan teeth, a green head tail/tuft, and a bone topper attached to that head tail with brown yarn. This cleaned Crafties version follows the supplied Prehistoric Om Nom sheet and adds the missing practical notes for spots, the head tail, mouth embroidery, and bone assembly.

What we are building

Prehistoric Om Nom is a pear-shaped green crochet plush worked in continuous rounds with a wide middle and a soft tapered top. The toy has two large white eyes, black pupils or safety eyes, a simple embroidered smile, exactly three tan teeth on the lower mouth line, two side arms, two base feet, dark green spots, a green head tail/tuft made from the same yarn as the body, and a bone-white topper attached to the head tail with brown yarn. Finished size is about 6.5 in / 16.5 cm tall and 7 in / 18 cm wide, not including the head tail and bone topper.

💚 Nath-style note: the supplied sheet already had the main round counts. I kept those counts, fixed the confusing teeth colour, added the missing dark-green spot instructions, and clarified the bone topper so it is easier to actually make.
