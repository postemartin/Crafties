Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished letter: about 4 to 6 in / 10 to 15 cm tall.
      Choose a simple letter shape for your first try: I, L, T, H, or O are easier than S or G.
      This is a decorative keepsake for a shelf, door, gift tag, or room sign.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Wrap the first letter of someone’s name and add a tiny tag that says one kind word about them.

            Safety and display

              For a wall or door hanger, keep the loop short and secure. For a gift topper, tie it to the ribbon after glue has fully dried.
