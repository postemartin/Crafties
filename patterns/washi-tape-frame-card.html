The look we are making

This professional version keeps the beginner-friendly idea, but gives it an adult stationery finish: muted washi tape, clean frame edges, a blank cream center, and a matching kraft envelope. The secret is measuring lightly, overlapping tiny corners, and pressing the tape flat.
