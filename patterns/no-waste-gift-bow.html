      Before you start

      Choose woven cotton scraps, shirt fabric, quilting cotton, or linen; very stretchy fabric is harder for a first bow.
      Press the fabric flat first if you can; crisp folds make the bow look tidy.
      Use small stitches and do not worry if the back is not perfect — gift bows are forgiving.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing and use

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Tie the bow onto a wrapped gift, then tuck in a tiny note that says: wrapped with love, not waste.

            Safety and care

              This reusable bow is meant for gift wrapping, baskets, and decorative packages. Store it flat after the celebration so it can sparkle again next time.
