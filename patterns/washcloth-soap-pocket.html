Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Use a clean, completely dry washcloth and a wrapped or fully cured dry soap bar.
      Do not cover the soap’s ingredient, allergen, maker, weight, or safety information.
      This pocket is removable packaging. Take ribbon, tags, pins, and paper off before using the soap.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing and gifting

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Add a tiny removable tag that says “A soft little reset for your day.” Keep the ingredient label visible beside it.

            Safety and care

              This washcloth soap pocket is a removable gift wrap for one dry soap bar. Remove all ribbon, tags, pins, elastics, paper, and decorative pieces before bathing or washing.
