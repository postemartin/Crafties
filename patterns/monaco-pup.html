Monaco Pup

A closer Crafties-style rewrite for the golden racing dachshund. This version now follows the card construction Nath spotted: the main pup is worked continuously from the muzzle, through the head and long body, and out to the little tail, so the pattern and picture tell the same story.

What we are keeping

The card reads as one smooth little dachshund shape from nose to tail. This rewrite keeps that feeling: no separate head sewn onto a separate body, and the tail is formed as part of the rear shaping. The only add-ons are the practical bits that naturally sit on top — ears, legs, harness, collar, and tiny details.

💜 Little Nath-style note: work the main pup like a soft tube with personality — muzzle first, then forehead, neck, long body, rump, and tail. Keep the stuffing even as you go so the back stays smooth instead of looking like separate joined pieces.

      Before you start

      Finished size is about 10 in / 25.5 cm long and roughly 6.5 in / 16.5 cm tall if you use bulky yarn and a 4.0 mm hook, or a little smaller with lighter yarn.
      To match the card better, think in one continuous path: muzzle, head, neck, long body, rump, and tail. The shape changes happen with increases and decreases instead of sewing a separate head onto a body.
      This page has been desk-tested for round counts and shaping logic so the written construction matches the card better. It has not been physically stitched in yarn by Hermy yet, so a hands-on sample may still need tiny comfort tweaks.
