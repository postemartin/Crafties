F1 Pup Prestige House

A prestige F1-inspired crochet dog house with a large open entrance, black ribbed roof, racing red body, cream-and-gold trim, checkered details, a cozy cushion, and a blank bone sign ready for the dog’s name.

Real dog-friendly opening

What makes it F1 prestige

This update removes the little human door and turns the project into a true dog house: a large open arch, firm supported panels, a sporty black roof, racing red body, cream-and-gold piping, and a bone sign ready for the dog’s name.

💜 Nath note: this is a desk-drafted crochet pattern, not physically test-stitched yet. For a real pup version, use covered removable support panels and measure your dog before starting.

      Before you start

      The front must stay open. Do not add a door, flap, button closure, or hanging decoration inside the entry.
      Work with firm tension. The F1 look depends on clean panels, straight seams, black ribbed roof texture, and neat cream/gold trim.
      For pet safety, all support pieces must be covered, smooth, and removable if the house needs washing.
