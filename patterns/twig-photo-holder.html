Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished size: about 6 to 8 in / 15 to 20 cm tall, depending on your twig.
      Twig size: choose one dry twig 6 to 8 in / 15 to 20 cm long and about pencil-thick.
      Best photo size: one small photo or card up to 3 x 4 in / 7.5 x 10 cm.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Clip in a tiny note that says why the photo makes you smile.

            Safety and display note

            Use clean, dry twigs only. This is a decorative photo holder, not a toy. If using hot glue, ask an adult to help and let the glue cool completely before handling.
