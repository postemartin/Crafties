      Before you start

      Choose smooth buttons with no sharp edges, cracks, rust, or loose shanks.
      Plan the bracelet flat on a towel before stringing so the button colors feel balanced.
      Make it comfortable, not tight: elastic bracelets should slide over the hand and rest gently on the wrist.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Pick one button to mean “you are loved exactly as you are,” then place it at the front center.

            Safety and care

              This beginner bracelet is a decorative accessory for careful wear. Check the knot and buttons before each use, and remove it before sleep, sports, bathing, swimming, or messy crafting.
