    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished necklace: about 18 to 20 in / 46 to 51 cm around, adjustable shorter for children.
      Make 18 to 24 paper beads for a light beginner necklace, plus 2 spare beads for practice.
      Let beads dry fully before wearing so the necklace does not feel sticky.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Choose one bead color to mean kindness, courage, joy, or calm, then tell the wearer what it means.

            Safety and wearing

              Paper beads are lightweight and beautiful, but they are not waterproof. Remove the necklace before bathing, swimming, or sleeping.
