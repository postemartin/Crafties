Pressed-Leaf Card

A polished botanical greeting card with real flat pressed leaves, cream cardstock, a kraft envelope, and careful glue placement so the finish stays delicate and giftable.

The look we are making

This professional version turns a simple leaf idea into a calm stationery piece: real pressed leaves, cream card, tiny hidden glue dots, and a matching kraft envelope. The trick is using fully dry leaves and pressing the finished card flat while it dries.
