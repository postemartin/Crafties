      Before you start

      Use super bulky yarn for a soft palm-sized heart, or worsted yarn with a smaller hook for a flatter appliqué.
      Keep your turning chains loose so the sides do not curl inward.
      Count after every row. The neat heart point depends on the stitch increases staying centered.
