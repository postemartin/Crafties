      Before you start

      Finished size: about 4 x 4 in / 10 x 10 cm after light blocking.
      Gauge is flexible: about 4 stitches per inch / 2.5 cm in garter stitch.
      Coasters work best in cotton yarn because cotton absorbs moisture and handles warm mugs well.

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Make a set of two and wrap them with a tiny note: “for slow sips and soft mornings.”

            Pattern note

            Count stitches at the end of each row, keep the side edges relaxed, and make the coaster a little longer or shorter if your gauge is different. Cotton yarn is best for real mug use because it absorbs moisture and tolerates gentle washing.
