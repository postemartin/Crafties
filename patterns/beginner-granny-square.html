      Before you start

      Finished size: about 5–6 in / 13–15 cm square with worsted yarn.
      Gauge is flexible for practice; keep your tension relaxed and even.
      Turn the square right side facing you after each round if it begins to twist.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Make two squares: keep one as your practice square and give one to a friend as a tiny blanket-starting blessing.

            Pattern note

            If your square waves, use a slightly smaller hook or fewer chain stitches between side clusters. If it cups, use a slightly larger hook, loosen your tension, or add a gentle blocking step before gifting.
