Om Nelle Crochet Amigurumi

A sweet lime-green amigurumi Om Nelle in the style of the beloved Om Nom character — but with a feminine twist! She has a round green body (doubled in size for extra huggability), four short stubby feet (two front, two back), large white eyes with black pupils, rosy pink cheeks, four tiny white teeth on the upper jaw, a single curved green antenna with a pink bow beside it, and a stitched smile. Worked in continuous rounds from the bottom up, she stands about 13 inches tall (with antenna) and 14 inches wide. An intermediate-level project full of personality.

About this pattern

Om Nelle is a round green amigurumi built entirely with single crochet in continuous spiral rounds. Her body is doubled in size for extra huggability — it starts at the bottom and grows upward, with four stubby feet worked separately and sewn on. The antenna, bow, large round eyes, pupils, pink cheeks, teeth, and smile are all added during assembly. All other pieces stay at the original size, giving her a big-body, small-features look. Unlike the classic Om Nom, this Om Nelle has no arms — just a round body with four little feet peeking underneath. The result is a soft, huggable character perfect for little hands.

💚 Little Nath-style note: Om Nelle's charm is in the face — take your time with the eyes, cheeks, and teeth. The body is doubled in size, so stuff it firmly in layers as you go so she keeps her round shape. Position all four feet evenly underneath so she sits flat and steady. The bigger body makes the small bow, eyes, and feet look extra cute by contrast.

      Before you start

      Finished size is approx 13 in / 33 cm tall (with antenna) and 14 in / 36 cm wide when using worsted weight cotton yarn and a 3.5 mm hook. The body is doubled from the original pattern — all other pieces stay the same size. Size may vary slightly depending on your tension and yarn choice.
      The body is worked in continuous spiral rounds from the bottom up. Use a stitch marker to keep track of the start of each round.
      All pieces except the body and feet are small surface details. Take your time with the face — it really brings Om Nelle to life!
