      Before you start

      Finished size: about 3 in / 7.5 cm across the flower.
      Petals: 5 outer petals and 5 inner petals, each cut from fabric.
      Stiff fabric holds the cupped petal shape best — silk, satin, or cotton work well.

            Pattern note

            This is a true printable hand-sewing pattern. The gold trim gives each petal a defined, luxurious edge. Take your time cupping the petals — a gentle fold at the base makes the flower look three-dimensional.
