Scrap Paper Rainbow Card

A refined torn-paper greeting card that turns leftover scraps into a soft arched rainbow — neat enough to gift, simple enough for beginners, and perfect for using the prettiest pieces from the craft drawer.

The look we are making

This professional version keeps the playful rainbow idea, but makes it feel calmer and more grown-up: cream cardstock, layered torn-paper arches, a quiet colour palette, and one matching envelope. The torn edges are meant to show — they are the handmade texture.

            Beginner fixes

              💜 Crafties touch: Hide one tiny purple or teal scrap in the rainbow as your quiet Team Crafties signature, then use the offcuts to decorate the envelope flap.

              Safety and care

                Desk-checked pattern: card size, arch order, glue sequence, envelope finish, and mailing notes were tightened to match the new finished card photo. For mailing, keep the rainbow layers flat and let the glue dry fully before sliding the card into the envelope.
