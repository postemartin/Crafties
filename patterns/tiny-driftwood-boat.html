Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished boat size: about 4 to 5 in / 10 to 13 cm long and 4 to 6 in / 10 to 15 cm tall.
      Use clean, dry wood only. Brush off sand or dirt and let beach wood dry overnight.
      This is a decorative display boat, not a floating bath toy.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Write a tiny wish on the sail, like “brave seas,” “soft days,” or “keep going.”

            Safety and display

              Let glue cure fully before moving the boat. Display it on a shelf, desk, tray, or windowsill away from water and small children.
