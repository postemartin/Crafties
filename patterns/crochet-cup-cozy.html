      Before you start

      Finished size: about 4 in / 10 cm tall and 9–10 in / 23–25 cm around before stretching.
      Fits many standard takeaway cups; check around your cup after Round 2.
      Work in joined rounds so the sleeve stays tidy and beginner-friendly.

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Tuck a small note inside before gifting: “for warm hands and a cozy heart.”

            Pattern note

            Cup sizes vary, so the best beginner habit is to try the cozy on your cup as you go. Add or remove 2 foundation chains for a better fit, and keep the cozy low enough that it never touches the drinking edge.
