Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished charm size: about 2½ to 3 in / 6.5 to 7.5 cm long, including hanging loop.
      Bead goal: make 5 paper beads, each about ¼ to ⅜ in / 6 to 10 mm wide.
      Drying time: let painted or sealed beads dry fully before stringing.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Choose one bead color to represent a kind wish, like calm blue, brave purple, or happy yellow.

            Safety and use

              This charm is decorative. Keep it dry, do not give it to small children, and avoid wearing it in the shower or rain.
