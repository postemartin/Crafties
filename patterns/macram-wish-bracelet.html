Materials

    Knots and terms

      Before you start

      Finished bracelet: adjustable, fits most wrists from about 6–8 in / 15–20 cm.
      Cut lengths: one center cord 24 in / 60 cm and one knotting cord 48 in / 120 cm.
      Best cord: waxed cotton, hemp, or nylon cord about 1 mm thick.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Choose a cord color that stands for the wish: purple for courage, pink for kindness, teal for calm.

            Safety and use

            This is a decorative bracelet, not a baby or pet item. Pull each knot snug but not painfully tight; relaxed, even knots make the bracelet comfortable. Trim or seal ends with grown-up help if using synthetic cord.
