Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Use a wrapped soap bar, boxed soap, or soap in its original label so ingredients stay visible.
      For the candle piece, use only an unlit wax tea-light for display. Do not light any candle inside or beside the paper sleeve.
      Keep the sleeve removable: nothing should be glued directly to soap, candle, or ingredient labels.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing and gift card

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Write a tiny spa card: “Tonight is for soft hands, warm tea, and one quiet breath.”

            Safety and care

              This gift sleeve is decorative packaging. Remove all paper, ribbon, tags, and decorations before using the soap, and keep the wax tea-light candle unlit while it is packaged with the gift.
