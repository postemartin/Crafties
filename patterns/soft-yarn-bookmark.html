      Before you start

      Use cotton or smooth acrylic yarn so the bookmark lies flat and does not shed heavily inside a book.
      Finished tassel length: about 5½–6 in / 14–15 cm; loop: about 2½–3 in / 6–7.5 cm.
      Keep beads small and smooth, or skip the bead for children, classrooms, or library-book gifts.
