    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished star: about 5 to 6 in / 13 to 15 cm across.
      Use five twigs that are close in length so the star points look balanced.
      Dry-fit the star on the table before tying any knots.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Tie on a tiny paper tag with one word like hope, cozy, joy, or brave before gifting or hanging.

            Safety and display

              This twig star is a decorative ornament. Hang it indoors or in a sheltered place; natural twigs and yarn may soften outdoors in rain or snow.
