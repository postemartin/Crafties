      Before you start

      Finished size: about 4 in / 10 cm diameter circle.
      Patchwork pieces: 4–6 fabric scraps in different colours, each at least 2 x 3 in / 5 x 7.5 cm.
      Backing: one circle of plain fabric or felt, 4 in / 10 cm diameter.

            Pattern note

            This is a true printable hand-sewing pattern. The patchwork look comes from stitching small fabric rectangles together, then cutting the joined piece into a circle. Each coaster is unique — that is the charm.
