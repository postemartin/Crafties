Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished ornament: about 4 to 5 in / 10 to 13 cm across.
      Use two clean, dry sticks close to the same length so the cross stays balanced.
      This is a decorative wall or window ornament, not a toy or outdoor wind chime.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Choose one color that reminds you of the person receiving it, then write that color meaning on a tiny tag.

            Safety and display

              Hang the finished weaving indoors where it will stay dry. The sticks and yarn can bend, fade, or grow mildew if left outside in rain or damp air.
