      Before you start

      This is a decorative brooch, not a toy. Adult supervision is required for children.
      Use new, smooth safety pins that close firmly and have no rust, bends, or sharp burrs.
      Work over a towel or tray so beads and open pins cannot roll away.

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Choose one bead color for courage, one for joy, and one for calm; write the meaning on a tiny backing card.

            Safety and care

              This beginner brooch page is made to print, practice, and share with a careful maker. Wear the finished brooch only on sturdy fabric and remove it before sleep, sports, rough play, washing, or hugging babies and pets.
