      Before you start

      Choose medium yarn that feels soft but not slippery; it is easier to trim into a round flower.
      Decide if your flower is for display, a card, or a bouquet before adding the stem.
      Trim slowly over a tray or paper towel; tiny yarn bits travel everywhere.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing and display

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Tie on a tiny removable tag that says “A bloom for your brave little day.”

            Safety and care

              This pom-pom flower is a decorative craft, not a toy. Keep long stems, yarn loops, wire, glue, and trimmed yarn fluff away from babies, toddlers, pets, and sleeping areas.
