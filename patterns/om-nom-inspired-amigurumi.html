Om Nom Crochet Plush

A round, lime-green amigurumi plush with big circular white eyes, four tiny crocheted teeth, and a mischievous curved smile. This hungry little monster works up in green worsted cotton yarn with a 3.5 mm hook — stitch the round body with Nath's exact stitch counts, add four stubby feet and a single antenna, then bring the silly sweet face to life with crocheted eyes, pupils, and teeth.

What we are building

Om Nom is a cheerful little round monster with a huge appetite for candy — and a huge personality for such a tiny creature. This amigurumi plush captures his signature lime-green spherical body, two large circular white eyes with black pupils placed high and inward (giving him that slightly cross-eyed look), four tiny crocheted white teeth on the upper jaw, a curved smile embroidered in black, four short stubby green feet, and a single curved green antenna on top. He measures about 14 inches/36 cm wide and 13 inches/33 cm tall including the antenna. The body is worked in continuous rounds from the bottom up, wider than it is tall, with Nath's exact stitch counts for a perfectly rounded sphere. The body is doubled in size from the original — all other pieces (feet, antenna, eyes, teeth) stay the same size, giving Om Nom his signature big-body, small-features look.

💚 Nath-style tip: the lime-green spherical body is the icon — and now it's doubled! Work the increases precisely to round 12 (72 sts), then work 10 even rounds for the plump middle, then decrease symmetrically. Stuff extra firmly at the base so Om Nom sits up on his own without wobbling. The bigger body makes the small feet, eyes, and teeth look even cuter by contrast.

                      Pattern note

                      This pattern uses Nath's exact stitch counts from her Om Nom infographic — with the body doubled in size.
