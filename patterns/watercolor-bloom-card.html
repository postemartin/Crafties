Materials

    Measurements and terms

      Before you start

      Finished card: 5½ x 4¼ in / 14 x 10.8 cm after folding.
      Watercolor panel: about 5 x 3¾ in / 12.7 x 9.5 cm after trimming or taping.
      Use less water than you think; beginner watercolor is easier with damp, not dripping, brushes.

      Pattern instructions

        Finishing

          Beginner help

            💜 Kindness twist: Add one tiny hidden heart, dot, or encouraging word among the leaves for the person receiving the card.

            Safety, drying, and mailing

              Let watercolor dry fully before folding, writing, stacking, or mailing. A dry card feels cool but not damp and should not transfer color to scrap paper.
